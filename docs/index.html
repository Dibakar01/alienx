alienX GitHub

alienX writes your X. You press post.

It finds ideas, writes in your voice, draws the picture and books the time. Nothing posts until you tap. Runs on your Mac. Free with your Claude plan.

Download for Mac
  1. 1Open the zip
  2. 2Double-click alienX
  3. 3Make your account handle, topics, writing style

First open only: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Needs a Mac with Node 24+, Google Chrome and the Claude Code CLI signed in.

Or paste this in Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dibakar01/alienx/main/install.sh | bash
